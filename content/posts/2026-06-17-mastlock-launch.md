## The problem no one talks about

Four screws. Two minutes minimum. One lost nut in the sand.

We've all been there. The water is perfect, the wind is right, and you're on your knees in the sand, fishing for a M6 bolt that just disappeared into the beach between your fingers. Your session window is closing.

It's not the hardware's fault. The existing mast-plate connection was designed before modern foil riding existed — it's an adaptation of a windsurfing standard that was never built for daily high-frequency use. It's fine for a once-a-week session. For riders going out multiple times a week, switching boards, or traveling with gear, it becomes a genuine source of friction.

We spent two years doing something about it.

## One grip. Locked.

MastLOCK replaces the entire screw-based connection with a single quarter-turn cam lever. Install it once on your baseplate. After that, mount and dismount with one hand in under ten seconds.

The mechanism is deceptively simple: an eccentric cam rotates through 90 degrees, generating 8kN of clamping force across a precision-machined datum surface. No springs, no ratchets, no consumable parts. The geometry does the work.

The audible click when the cam seats tells you you're locked — no torque wrench required.

## Engineering the mundane

Most of the development time wasn't spent on the cam mechanism. That part was solved early. The hard work was everything else:

**Repeatability.** We needed the mast to return to the exact same position every mount — same angle, same depth, same lateral alignment. Sub-millimeter repeatability, session after session, board after board. This is what pump foilers specifically asked for. When your body has memorized a foot position, you cannot accept variance.

**Shimming.** Every rider has a preferred trim angle. We wanted to preserve the ability to tune that angle precisely, without tools, without guesswork. The 0.5mm aluminium shim system stacks behind the baseplate and locks in your preferred setup. Once set, it stays set.

**Weight.** The whole system is 85 grams. Adding it to an existing setup is a net-neutral for any serious rider.

**Compatibility.** Every ISO-spec mast track. Every board. The market is fragmented enough; we weren't going to make you choose.
